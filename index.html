The Last Tram

Original text created for this public demonstration.
Reading focus: How does the writer construct a voice that feels both observant and uncertain? Highlight useful evidence, then build an interpretation in the panel.

By the time the last tram arrived, the rain had polished the rails into two bright lines. Mara stood beneath the timetable pretending to read it, although she knew every departure by heart.

Across the road, the bakery was dark except for a square of yellow light above the stairs. Someone was still awake up there, moving from room to room, living a life she could only measure in shadows.

The tram doors opened with a tired sigh. She stepped forward, stopped, and looked once more at the street behind her. It seemed smaller than it had that morning.
